🗺️ Kharita: NASA Earth Science & Field Guide

Both the global Earth simulation and the 5-room RPG reflect real orbital instruments from NASA's Earth Information Center (EIC):

  • OCO-2 & OCO-3 (Orbiting Carbon Observatory): Measures atmospheric CO₂ from space to map emissions and forest carbon sinks.
  • GEDI (Global Ecosystem Dynamics Investigation): Space Station LiDAR laser mapping 3D forest canopy height and stored biomass.
  • SMAP (Soil Moisture Active Passive): Tracks soil water worldwide to monitor droughts, floods, and soil microbiomes.
  • PACE (Plankton, Aerosol, Cloud, ocean Ecosystem): Watches ocean color to track phytoplankton that generate ~50% of Earth's oxygen.
  • SWOT (Surface Water and Ocean Topography): Surveys Earth's rivers, lakes, and coastal sea-surface heights.

Ocean Acidification & Reef Chemistry

CO₂ + H₂O ⇌ H₂CO₃ ⇌ H⁺ + HCO₃⁻

Excess atmospheric CO₂ dissolves into seawater to form carbonic acid (H₂CO₃), releasing H⁺ ions that lower ocean pH from its healthy ~8.1–8.2 baseline.

CaCO₃ (Coral Skeleton) + H⁺ → Ca²⁺ + HCO₃⁻

Higher acidity makes it harder for corals and shellfish to build calcium carbonate skeletons.

Photosynthesis & Carbon Sinks

6CO₂ + 6H₂O + Sunlight → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂

Forests, meadows, and phytoplankton convert CO₂ into oxygen and organic carbon locked in roots and deep soil mycelium.

Radiative Forcing

ΔF ≈ 5.35 × ln(C / C₀) W/m²

Each doubling of CO₂ above pre-industrial levels (C₀ = 280 ppm) traps roughly 3.7 W/m² of extra infrared heat.

Key Earth & Climate Milestones

  • 1760 — Industrial Revolution: Coal-powered industry begins raising atmospheric CO₂ from ~280 ppm.
  • 1958 — The Keeling Curve: Charles David Keeling begins precision CO₂ measurements at Mauna Loa (315 ppm).
  • 1970 — Clean Air Act: Landmark policy cuts sulfur dioxide and soot, proving clean air regulations work.
  • 1987 — Montreal Protocol: Global treaty phases out ozone-depleting CFCs so the ozone layer can heal.
  • 2015 — Paris Agreement: Nations unite to pursue limiting global warming to 1.5 °C above pre-industrial levels.
